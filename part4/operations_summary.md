# Which orders should support contact?

Open the follow-up list and start at the top. Contact only the customers marked for follow-up. A team with 100 newly approved orders can contact up to ten. Offer a helpful order update and record whether the customer needed help.

Open the summary generated in `artifacts/operations_summary.md` for the current run. It shows how many customers with negative reviews the list found, how many flagged orders had reviews, and the latest month's contact limit. Customers who do not leave a review still count toward the limit.

Before expanding outreach, compare a small randomly chosen group receiving a call with another group receiving usual service. If the latest month has very few reviews or the order feed has stopped updating, investigate before relying on the ranking.

In the verified public benchmark, a 10% contact limit found about 13 of every 100 orders with a negative review. About 14 of every 100 flagged orders with reviews had a negative review. September contained one eligible order, so the limit allowed zero contacts. Measure whether outreach changes outcomes in a controlled trial.
